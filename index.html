Our Mission

The heartbeat of the Marine Corps League is the local detachment. We band together honorably discharged Marines, FMF Corpsmen, and Navy Chaplains to ensure we take care of our own, support local veteran programs, and remain vital citizen-partners in our community.

Preserve Traditions

Celebrating the history, birthdays, and lineage of the United States Marine Corps.

Marines Helping Marines

Providing direct support to local veterans and their families in times of need.

Community Strength

Participating in local civic events, fundraisers, and youth programs.
